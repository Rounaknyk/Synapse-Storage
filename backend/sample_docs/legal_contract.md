# Sample Legal Document

## SERVICE AGREEMENT

### Parties
This Agreement is entered into between:
- **Party A**: Tech Solutions Inc.
- **Party B**: Digital Services LLC

### Terms and Conditions

#### 1. Scope of Services
The Contractor agrees to provide software development services as outlined in this contract. All deliverables must meet the specifications agreed upon by both parties.

#### 2. Payment Terms
- Total Contract Value: $50,000
- Payment Schedule: Monthly installments of $10,000
- Payment Method: Wire transfer within 15 days of invoice

#### 3. Liability Clause
Both parties agree to limit liability to direct damages only. Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from this agreement.

#### 4. Confidentiality Clause
Both parties agree to maintain strict confidentiality regarding all proprietary information shared during the term of this contract.

#### 5. Term and Termination
- Contract Duration: 6 months
- Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice
- Upon termination, all outstanding payments become due immediately

#### 6. Governing Law
This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.

#### 7. Dispute Resolution
Any disputes arising from this contract shall be resolved through binding arbitration.
